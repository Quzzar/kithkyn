Precise joinery and tactile wit, like an accomplished independent game studio. No literal wood board behind the wordmark.

# Reference-led refinement: Offcut

Create one original transparent PNG logo lockup for Kithkyn, an independent Minecraft mod about autonomous villagers building their own settlements. Exact brand text: Kithkyn (one capital K, then lowercase ithkyn). This is an identity study, not a game scene. We want understated playful timber craftsmanship in the LOGO, while the website will be clean and modern. Horizontal lockup: a compact icon on the left, custom readable wordmark on the right. Icon: a simple hand-cut oak beam or offcut cleverly shaped into a K, robust clear silhouette, a little charming asymmetry. Wordmark: original chunky carved lettering, lightly irregular as if neatly cut from wood, dark slate-blue ink faces with a very thin warm oak edge. Letter shapes feel made by a game artist, not a standard startup sans font and not the Minecraft title font. Highly controlled flat 2D artwork, crisp shape boundaries, simple broad forms, almost no texture, one or two fine wood grain accents on the icon only. Palette: warm desaturated oak and dark slate-blue. Strong readability at small sizes. A restrained sense of slapstick from one slightly tipped beam, no cartoon face in this variant. Transparent background. Center the tightly composed logo with modest transparent padding. No scenery, no board behind the whole wordmark, no rope, no nails, no banner, no plaque, no gradients, no glossy bevels, no 3D extrusion, no fake weathering, no slogan or other text. Do not copy another mod logo.
