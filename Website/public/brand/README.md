# Provisional KithKyn logo exports

The Hearth and horizon website direction was rejected on September 30, 2026. These earlier
logo exports are retained as provisional source assets while three new website directions
are evaluated. They are not the chosen final identity. Do not restore the removed generated
village illustration or social banner. The previous downloadable kit has been removed.

The folder retains primary, reversed, monochrome, wordmark and emblem PNG originals,
matching WebP exports and icons at 32, 64, 180 and 512 pixels. Raster assets retain real
transparency; they are not vectors. Exact logo-generation prompts are recorded in
`generation-prompts.md`. Fonts retain their own SIL Open Font Licenses.

The mod-list logo currently uses the earlier hearth emblem. Any further identity change
should happen after a direction is selected. This project remains GPL-3.0-only.
