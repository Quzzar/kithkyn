# KithKyn wooden sign identity

Direction A was selected on September 30, 2026: a playful game title screen with crooked
wooden planks and painted pixel lettering. The name is KithKyn, with both Ks capitalized.

## Files

- Primary: warm oak sign with outlined pale lettering, subtle grain and two fasteners.
- Reversed: pale lettering and an oak edge on dark timber.
- Monochrome: one-color stamp with transparent lettering cutouts.
- Wordmark: pale outlined lettering without the board, for dark backgrounds.
- Emblem: a K on a small wooden signpost.
- Each variant has editable SVG source, transparent PNG and lossless WebP exports.
- PNG logos are 1880 pixels wide; the emblem is 1024 pixels square.
- Icons are 32, 64, 180 and 512 pixels square.
- The sharing card is 1200 × 630, with SVG source and PNG export.
- `menu-plank.svg` is the companion wooden button shape.
- Locally hosted Pixelify Sans and Outfit fonts retain their SIL Open Font Licenses.

The SVG logo lettering is outlined geometry and needs no installed font. The artwork is
code-authored vector geometry. There is no generated scene illustration in this kit.

## Use

Keep the logo proportions. Leave at least one painted-letter stroke of clear space around
the sign. Use the emblem at small sizes, the reversed sign on dark surfaces, and the
monochrome stamp for single-color output. Do not add shadows to the stamp or redraw the
lettering with a substitute font.

The palette is Forest #172d27, Oak #c58a4c, Paint #fff3d8, Timber #4f301d, Leaf #a9bb94,
and Paper #f1e3c5. Pixelify Sans sets headings; Outfit sets readable body text and controls.
The project is GPL-3.0-only. Font licenses are included separately.

The former generated village diorama and its banner derivative were rejected and removed.
They are not included in these exports. The current sharing card uses only the new logo.
